Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This page describes, plainly and accurately, what information tylerjanczak.com and its features actually collect, why, and where it goes.

What this site is

tylerjanczak.com is a personal portfolio site. It includes an AI assistant ("Tyler AI"), a scheduling feature, a resume-request feature, and standard web analytics. This policy covers all of them.

Information collected automatically

When you visit any page on this site, the following may be collected automatically:

  • IP address — used for basic security (e.g. blocking abusive visitors) and approximate location context for analytics.
  • Device and browser characteristics — such as screen size, time zone, browser type, and similar technical signals, combined into a non-identifying technical fingerprint used for security and abuse prevention.
  • Usage data — pages visited, session duration, and interactions, via Microsoft Clarity (see "Analytics" below).
Tyler AI (the chat assistant)

If you use the chat assistant, additionally collected:

  • The questions you ask and the answers given, stored to improve the assistant and for Tyler's own visibility into visitor interest.
  • A session identifier, used to maintain conversation context and prevent abuse.
  • If you request the resume or ask to schedule a call: your name, phone number, and/or email address, which you provide voluntarily.

Questions sent to Tyler AI are processed by OpenAI to generate responses. OpenAI's own privacy practices govern that processing; see OpenAI's privacy policy.

A note on moderation: if a conversation is flagged for abusive or inappropriate content, or an attempt to manipulate the assistant's instructions, the associated IP address and device fingerprint may be recorded and, after repeated violations, blocked from further access to the assistant.
Scheduling a call

If you request to schedule a call, real-time availability is pulled from Tyler's Calendly account. Your name, phone number, and email (which you provide) are sent via email to Tyler through Resend, a transactional email service, so he can follow up and confirm the meeting. No booking is made automatically without Tyler's direct confirmation.

Requesting the resume

If you request Tyler's resume, the email address you provide is used solely to send the resume via Resend, and is logged so Tyler can see that the request occurred.

Analytics

This site uses Microsoft Clarity for analytics, including session recordings and heatmaps of how visitors interact with the site. Clarity may set cookies and collect the technical information described above. See Microsoft's Privacy Statement for details on how Clarity handles data.

Cookies and browser storage

This site uses browser session storage (not persistent cookies in the traditional sense) to remember things like your current chat session and whether you've already seen certain page animations, so they don't unnecessarily repeat. This data is cleared when you close your browser tab and is not used to track you across other websites.

How long information is kept

Conversation logs, resume requests, and call requests are retained so Tyler can review visitor interest over time. Temporary session tokens used to access the chat expire automatically after a short window (currently 10 minutes) and cannot be reused. IP or device blocks from abuse are retained until manually reversed.

What this site does not do
  • It does not sell your information to anyone.
  • It does not use your information for third-party advertising.
  • It does not attempt to determine your real-world identity from technical fingerprinting alone — that data is used only for security and abuse prevention.
Your choices

You can decline to provide your name, phone, or email at any point, though doing so means features like resume delivery or call scheduling won't be able to complete. Most browsers let you block third-party cookies and clear site data at any time, including the analytics described above.

Contact
